# UAT — Double-COD Batch 1

Acceptance:
- reseller can create an order against active product economics;
- supplier settlement is explicitly recorded;
- supplier handoff and inventory movement are explicitly recorded;
- customer delivery/handoff/payment are separate milestones;
- realized margin is calculated from actual customer payment and supplier settlement;
- transaction history preserves pricing snapshots;
- cancellation/exception records do not erase historical events.
